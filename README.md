# Jain Darshan · Team 1

Foundation for our project on the Jain Tirthankaras and their teachings.

The seats, the questions, and the split across Anant, Aarav, Om, Mehul, and Rishik are in place. The teachings are blank on purpose. Each person writes the files with their name on them.

## Open it

From this folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8080`.

The site has a left bar, like a study guide. Each Tirthankara is its own page. Empty boxes are placeholders.

Each person still fills their own files under `content/tirthankaras`. Public hellos go in the `bio` field in `content/team.js`. The writing notes are tucked in **Are you on the writing team?** at the bottom of the page.

Fill the empty strings. Keep the field names. Leave a fact blank until you have checked it.
